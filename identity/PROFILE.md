# Amir Profile

## Purpose

This file describes how I learn, communicate, and work with AI assistance.

The AI should use this information to provide better guidance, explanations, and support.

---

# Learning Style

I learn best when:

* Concepts are explained in simple terms first.
* Real-world analogies are used when helpful.
* Technical explanations come after the basic understanding.
* I understand why something works, not just what steps to follow.
* I can connect concepts to practical examples.

I learn especially well through:

* Hands-on projects.
* Troubleshooting real problems.
* Building systems.
* Breaking things and understanding why they broke.
* Visualizations that help 3d project it in my mind

---

# Current Technical Journey

My primary goal is becoming a stronger technical professional, especially in areas related to:

* Technical Support Engineering
* Customer Support Engineering
* Infrastructure
* Networking
* Cybersecurity
* Linux
* APIs
* Docker/Containerism/VMs etc
* Cloud technologies
* Programming fundamentals

I want to develop the ability to understand computers deeply, from basic concepts to advanced systems.

---

# AI Interaction Preferences

I prefer AI to:

* Be direct and honest.
* Explain assumptions.
* Challenge my thinking when necessary.
* Point out tradeoffs.
* Help me avoid unnecessary complexity.
* Teach me instead of only solving problems for me.

I do not want an AI that simply agrees with everything.

I want an AI that helps me improve.

---

# Project Philosophy

I prefer:

* Practical learning over passive studying.
* Building real systems over only reading theory.
* Understanding fundamentals over memorizing commands.
* Long-term skill development over quick shortcuts.

Projects should create useful experience and evidence of growth.

---

# Decision Making

When evaluating ideas, consider:

* Does this help my long-term goals?
* Does this build useful skills?
* Does this create meaningful progress?
* Is this the right time?

Interesting ideas should be captured, but not every idea deserves immediate execution.

---

# Communication Style

I appreciate:

* Clear explanations.
* Step-by-step guidance.
* Examples.
* Analogies.
* Technical depth when appropriate.

Avoid:

* Assuming I already know advanced concepts.
* Overly academic explanations without context.
* Unnecessary complexity.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Build myself into someone who can confidently understand, troubleshoot, design, and create technology systems.

Use AI as a learning multiplier while continuing to develop independent understanding and capability.
